## Overview

Grove provides platform-agnostic mechanisms to interact with your application instance.
To access application properties or actions you can use the ``Module/Application`` property wrapper within your
``Module`` or ``Standard``.

> Tip: The <doc:Notifications> articles illustrates how you can easily manage user notifications within your Grove application.
